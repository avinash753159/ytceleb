# Chris Hemsworth — source map

Built to satisfy the owner's YouTube rule: **one clip per source video**, no
clip over 10 seconds, no repeats. The previous film took 8 bites from a single
Rogan episode; under this rule it needed 14 sources. So the sources come first
and the script is written against them.

---

## The thesis changed during research

The plan was "the man who plays a god found out he has a countdown". That is
true but it is not the film. The real one:

> **Chris Hemsworth carries two copies of APOE4 — eight to ten times the
> population risk of Alzheimer's. His grandfather had it. His father Craig,
> 71, has it now. In 2025 he drove his father around Australia to visit the
> places tied to Craig's memories, while Craig was losing them.**

He is not a man told about a risk. He is a man watching his own future happen
to his father, and filming it.

That reframes the fitness content entirely. The training is not vanity and it
is not longevity theatre — it is the one lever he has been told might matter,
applied by a man who has seen where the road goes.

---

## The rule that makes this easy

**A print source needs no clip at all.** A quote from Vanity Fair becomes a
`source_highlight` — the page treated as a physical object, the sentence marked
— or a `headline_over`. No footage, no copyright exposure, no 10-second limit,
and it satisfies rule F5 (show the actual page) at the same time.

So the map splits in two: a small number of **video** sources supplying sync
bites, and a larger number of **print** sources supplying quotes as documents.
Only the video ones consume the one-clip-per-source budget.

## What the record does NOT support

- **No published training split tied to the Alzheimer's response.** Centr has
  programmes; the shift to "longevity training" is described in interviews but
  not published as a prescription. Say what he says; invent nothing.
- **Exercise does not treat or prevent Alzheimer's.** Nothing in this record
  claims it does and the film must not imply it. This is the same discipline
  that governed the Crohn's film.
- **Craig is a private individual with a diagnosis.** He consented to a
  documentary; that is not consent to be a prop. Use him once, in his son's
  frame, with dignity, or not at all.

---

## Consequences for the script

1. **Five sync moments, maximum.** The rest of the film is narration over
   generated picture and document treatments. That is cheaper *and* compliant.
2. **The document treatments carry the evidence.** Twelve print quotes, each a
   `source_highlight` at $0. This is where the new effects earn their place.
3. **The turn is not the gene, it is the father.** The gene is act one. Craig
   is act two. What Chris does about it is act three.
4. **The training content lands in act three**, as a response rather than a
   topic — which is the only way it is honest, and the only way it is
   interesting.
