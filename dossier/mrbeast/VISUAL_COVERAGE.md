## Editorial rule

The visual spine must remain primary evidence. Use MrBeast-owned archive,
Jimmy's own interview image, the Airrack challenge footage in which Jimmy
participates, the original 2023 transformation post, and authoritative medical
documents before generic stock. A candidate window below is not cleared for
the timeline until the full source is downloaded and a three-frame/scene-
boundary check confirms it.

Do not imply that:

- Airrack's exercises, trainer, meals, or bodybuilding prep were Jimmy's.
- the 2024 Chris Hemsworth circuit was Jimmy's 2022-23 fat-loss program.
- the June 2023 before/after photographs have known capture dates.
- the 600-day Airrack challenge and Jimmy's earlier 310-day statement are one
  uninterrupted, identically defined contract.
- Minecraft footage shows Jimmy's body. It proves only early channel activity.

## Final visual QC gates

For each selected window:

1. Download the complete source, not a search-result excerpt.
2. Run scene detection and constrain each cut to one shot.
3. Inspect start/middle/end frames after crop and color treatment.
4. Reject third-party watermarks, reaction overlays, fan captions, title
   cards, vertical blur-fill, and blurry upscales.
5. Track source reuse: maximum three visually distinct cuts per source in the
   normal rule; Airrack may exceed this only across genuinely separate scenes
   and after a contact-sheet review.
6. Calculate final footage ratio; at least 60% of footage beats in chapters
   3–6 should visibly show Jimmy training, walking, physique evidence, or the
   primary fitness documents.
7. Check every claim-bearing image against its date and caption. A later
   workout can humanize or resolve; it cannot retroactively prove the earlier
   routine.
