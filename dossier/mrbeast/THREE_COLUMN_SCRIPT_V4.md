# The Disease That Built MrBeast | His Real Workout & Diet

Three-column radio script — Version 4, story-led rewrite.

## The spine (one sentence, everything serves it)

**At fifteen, Crohn's disease took Jimmy Donaldson's body away from him. He
spent the next decade building a machine that controlled everything else —
and the real story is what it cost to take his body back, and how fast he
lost it again.**

Not "a video about Crohn's." Not "a video about his workout." One story:
**losing control of your own body, and the repeated, unglamorous work of
reclaiming it.** Every clip and every line either advances that or is cut.

## Rules this version fixes (V3 broke all four)

1. **The cold open must not preview Act 1.** V3 opened on the weight-collapse
   clip and then the first chapter said the same thing again. The cold open
   now uses `doaceo_extreme_ambition`, which appears nowhere else, and it
   poses the question instead of answering it.
2. **Narration must ADD, never echo.** V3's worst moment: a clip stating the
   step count, immediately followed by narration stating the step count. No
   narration line here restates a number, a name, or a fact the adjacent clip
   just delivered. It supplies consequence, context, or contradiction.
3. **Music from 0:00.** V3's first cue began at 58.2 seconds and the cold-open
   chapter had no cue at all. Every act now has its own cue and the film
   opens scored.
4. **Despair to rise, with an honest fall.** The arc is: loss → substitution →
   cost → decision → rise → the method failing → losing it again → what he
   actually got to keep.
