# MrBeast documentary production standard

Working title: **The Disease That Built MrBeast | His Real Workout & Diet**

This file converts the channel's complete YouTube video system into binding
requirements for this film. It supplements the V11 technical gates and the
Celeb Workout feedback ledger. When requirements conflict, factual accuracy,
source verification, intelligibility, and story clarity win.

## 1. One-sentence story

**A creator who built an empire by controlling every variable wants to reclaim
his health and body, but Crohn's disease forces him to confront the one system
his obsession cannot fully control.**

Deeper version:

**This appears to be about MrBeast's workout and diet, but it is really about
control versus vulnerability—and the difference between managing a body and
defeating a disease.**

Creative filter:

- Keep facts that deepen control, vulnerability, obsession, accountability,
  bodily autonomy, or the cost of the transformation.
- Compress or remove business history that does not cause a later health or
  character beat.
- Never imply that training or diet cured, treated, or defeated Crohn's.

## 2. Click package

### Title

`The Disease That Built MrBeast | His Real Workout & Diet`

The title supplies:

- recognizable subject;
- hidden cause;
- contradiction;
- transformation;
- concrete viewer utility.

### Thumbnail brief

One idea: **the world's most controlling creator confronting a body he could
not control.**

- MrBeast is the only face and dominant focal subject.
- Use a verified gaunt teenage image contrasted with a verified later
  transformation image, or one split portrait if provenance and dates are
  clear.
- One medical/physical contradiction only; no collage.
- Maximum four visual elements.
- Zero to four words. Preferred test copy: `190 → 139` or `DAY 310`.
- The title gives the disease/workout promise; the thumbnail gives the bodily
  stakes or scale without repeating the title.
- Must remain readable at 10% working size.
- No fabricated hospital imagery, scars, body metrics, or expressions.

Three thumbnail variants must be built and tested at small size before the
final render is declared complete.

## 3. Opening contract: first 90 seconds

The opening must begin with an anomaly, not childhood chronology.

### 0:00–0:05 — visual anomaly

Show fifteen-year-old Jimmy on camera, visibly thin, behaving like an ordinary
teen creator.

Provisional line:

> He's fifteen here. What looks like an awkward old upload is also evidence.

### 0:05–0:20 — normal expectation versus reality

Normal expectation: a teenager playing baseball and building a YouTube channel.

Reality: he says his weight fell from roughly 190 to 139 pounds, he lost his
muscle, and baseball ended.

Use Jimmy's verified `DOACEO 13:47.5–13:55.1` source voice. Narration must not
repeat his numbers afterward.

### 0:20–0:40 — escalation

Cause-and-effect escalation:

Crohn's symptoms → eight to ten bathroom trips daily → food not being absorbed
normally → severe pain and rapid weight loss → treatment burden.

Use two short source quotations, separated by specific archival evidence.

### 0:40–0:55 — central question

Provisional line:

> Years later, he would build the most disciplined body transformation of his
> life. But was this just weight loss—or an attempt to reclaim the one thing
> his success had never allowed him to control?

### 0:55–1:30 — thesis montage

Promise the full range without giving away the meaning:

- verified teenage archive;
- `DAY 310`;
- the workout contract and tattoo consequence;
- walking during calls;
- verified training imagery;
- current treatment reality;
- the contrast between channel machinery and biological vulnerability.

Include one memorable Crohn's quotation and one training quotation. End on the
unresolved control-versus-vulnerability thesis.

## 4. Runtime and structural percentages

Target runtime: **14–18 minutes**, unless the radio edit proves a shorter film
is stronger.

| Portion | Function | MrBeast implementation |
|---|---|---|
| 0–7% | Hook and promise | The recorded bodily anomaly |
| 7–20% | Origin and desire | Baseball, illness, lost physical control |
| 20–40% | Progress and initial success | Obsession becomes a channel-building system |
| 40–55% | First reversal | Success does not restore health or bodily agency |
| 55–75% | Escalating complications | Contract, time cost, food, steps, stress |
| 75–90% | Crisis/confrontation | What the protocol can change—and what it cannot |
| 90–98% | Payoff and meaning | Reclaiming agency without claiming victory over Crohn's |
| Final 2% | Next viewing opportunity | Brief thematic bridge, no generic CTA speech |

## 5. Chapter question chain

Every chapter must pass all five fields before scripting is locked.

### Chapter 1 — The Kid Who Was Disappearing

- **Entry question:** What are we seeing in Jimmy's teenage videos that viewers
  could not understand at the time?
- **Discovery:** He says Crohn's arrived at fifteen and his weight fell from
  about 190 to 139 pounds.
- **Status change:** Baseball and his earlier physical identity collapse.
- **Partial payoff:** The early archive becomes an unintended medical record.
- **Exit question:** What did he build after losing control of his body?

### Chapter 2 — Ten Times a Day

- **Entry question:** What did the illness actually do to his daily life?
- **Discovery:** His first-person symptom and treatment account, carefully
  framed with authoritative medical context.
- **Status change:** The obstacle becomes chronic rather than a one-time loss.
- **Partial payoff:** We understand the physical stakes and vulnerability.
- **Exit question:** Why did this loss produce greater control everywhere else?

### Chapter 3 — The Machine

- **Entry question:** How did vulnerability become an operating system?
- **Discovery:** Jimmy's own language about obsession, standards, time, and
  accountability.
- **Status change:** The trait that isolates him also builds extraordinary
  success.
- **Partial payoff:** Control works spectacularly on videos and businesses.
- **Exit question:** What happens when he applies the same machinery to himself?

### Chapter 4 — The Contract

- **Entry question:** How do you force consistency when motivation is not
  enough?
- **Discovery:** The 310-day pact, tattoo consequence, programmed rest, and
  mutual accountability.
- **Status change:** Fitness becomes a system with external stakes.
- **Partial payoff:** The streak is not motivational folklore; its mechanism is
  documented.
- **Exit question:** What did the system demand every day?

### Chapter 5 — The Protocol

- **Entry question:** What did Jimmy verifiably do?
- **Discovery:** Approximately ninety minutes of training, food-related time,
  15,000 steps at the interview date, and calls taken while walking. The
  separate 12,500-step public claim appears only if the original post is
  acquired.
- **Status change:** The “simple” plan consumes at least two hours that
  previously went to the main channel.
- **Partial payoff:** The real protocol is consistency and environmental
  design—not a secret exercise.
- **Exit question:** What did all that control actually buy him?

### Chapter 6 — The Limit

- **Entry question:** Did the transformation solve the original problem?
- **Discovery:** Fitness changes body composition and agency; Crohn's remains a
  chronic disease requiring individualized medical care.
- **Status change:** The apparent victory becomes more complicated.
- **Partial payoff:** The workout is a biography of agency, not a cure.
- **Exit question:** If control cannot guarantee health, what was reclaimed?

### Chapter 7 — Reclaiming the Body

- **Entry question:** What did day 310 mean beyond a streak?
- **Discovery:** He converted calls, time pressure, friendship, and penalties
  into a durable behavioral system.
- **Status change:** The body is no longer merely where the original loss
  happened; it becomes a place where choice is possible again.
- **Partial payoff:** Answer the title honestly.
- **Exit question:** What other public transformation conceals a private
  conflict? This becomes the next-video bridge.

## 6. Research database requirements

Every factual story row must include:

- date;
- event;
- source and URL;
- exact primary quotation or document text;
- exact source time range;
- visual evidence;
- conflict;
- consequence;
- reliability: `fact`, `first-person claim`, `expert context`, or `inference`;
- possible chapter;
- sound opportunity;
- verification state.

Priority:

1. direct recording;
2. first-person interview;
3. original document/data/post;
4. contemporary reporting;
5. expert analysis;
6. retrospective commentary;
7. general summary.

Narration connects primary evidence. It does not replace available primary
evidence.

## 7. Script format and causality

The master script must be a three-column artifact:

| Picture/evidence | Speech | Sound |
|---|---|---|
| What the viewer learns visually | Narration or exact source voice | Dominant audio layer and transition |

Each column must add different information. Do not show, narrate, and caption
the same fact.

Every sequence should express:

`Because → decision → partial solution → new problem`

Prefer causal connectors such as `but`, `therefore`, `because`, `until`,
`despite`, `instead`, and `consequently`. Remove chronology that has no causal
effect on the transformation.

## 8. Narration rules

- Conversational passages: 135–165 WPM.
- Fast montage: 165–185 WPM.
- Emotional/medical passages: 110–140 WPM.
- Typical sentence: 8–18 words.
- Consequential statement: occasionally 2–7 words.
- New narrative beat every 20–45 seconds.
- Meaningful pause after major lines: 0.5–2 seconds.
- Narration may establish context, connect cause and consequence, compress
  time, direct attention to evidence, or frame the next question.
- Narration must not describe visible action or paraphrase a quotation the
  viewer just heard.
- Source voices deliver confessions, bodily experience, promises,
  contradictions, high-value numbers, and character-revealing language.

## 9. Radio edit gate

No picture assembly begins until an audio-only radio edit exists, is delivered
as a complete MP3/WAV, and the user explicitly signs off on the sound. This is
now a mandatory approval gate for every new CelebWorkout video, not just a
MrBeast review step. The radio edit must contain:

- draft narration;
- essential interview excerpts;
- intentional pauses;
- temporary chapter score;
- chapter transitions.

For narration-led documentaries, narration must be the majority of the measured
timeline. Interview clips are evidence and character punctuation; they must not
be allowed to carry the story merely because source footage is available.

Audio-only review questions:

- Is every person introduced?
- Does each chapter change Jimmy's situation or our understanding?
- Does every 2–4 minutes produce a meaningful partial payoff?
- Are any facts or quotes repeated?
- Do quotations reveal character rather than merely provide information?
- Can the reversals be felt without visuals?
- Does the ending answer the opening anomaly?
- Where does attention drift?

The radio edit must pass V11's real-voice ratio and silence/beat gates before
picture production.

## 10. Sound map

| Chapter/function | Music direction | Production sound/atmosphere | Transition |
|---|---|---|---|
| Opening observation | Minimal, unresolved | Old camera/room tone where authentic | Source voice enters before picture |
| Illness discovery | Restrained, low density | Interview room, subtle physical quiet | Subtract layers before key number |
| Channel machine | Precise rhythmic pulse | Keyboards, sets, production motion | Rhythm grows through causal montage |
| Contract/progress | Confident momentum | Gym effort, footsteps, phone-call motion | Impact only on the irreversible pact |
| Protocol | Controlled, practical | Plates, footsteps, weights, breathing | Music changes when cost appears |
| Limit/reversal | Remove rhythm, add space | Treatment/room ambience only if authentic | Near-silence before “not a cure” |
| Resolution | Warmth and gradual expansion | Return to early archive room tone | Early sound returns with new meaning |

Six available layers:

1. narration;
2. primary dialogue;
3. production sound;
4. music;
5. atmosphere;
6. designed effects.

Only one or two layers should dominate at a time.

Music cues require documented entrance, development, peak, exit, and handoff.
No cue continues merely because the track is long.

## 11. Mix requirements

- Draft and final voice generated once per locked script stage. For the new
  audio-first workflow, the complete final-voice radio cut is approved before
  any visual asset acquisition or picture assembly.
- Narration at 48 kHz in the assembly pipeline.
- Music ducking beneath complex speech begins in the 6–14 dB range and is
  adjusted by ear.
- Reduce percussion/melodic density beneath explanations.
- Preserve J-cuts and L-cuts for source dialogue; starting overlap 0.5–3
  seconds where structurally useful.
- V11 audio joins remain butt-joined with edge fades; never audio-crossfade
  chunks or reintroduce cumulative A/V drift.
- Check phone speakers, quiet playback, and mono.
- Final target: approximately −14 LUFS integrated and −1 dBTP, with dialogue
  intelligibility taking precedence.
- Silence is normally created by subtraction and room tone, not dead digital
  silence.

## 12. Visual evidence and pacing

Priority:

`actual event footage > primary evidence > specific contextual B-roll >
purposeful reconstruction/animation > metaphor > generic stock`

Every shot must prove, explain, locate, humanize, contrast, foreshadow, recall,
or transition.

Starting shot durations:

- energetic montage: 0.5–2.5 seconds;
- explanatory B-roll: 2–6 seconds;
- documents/maps/diagrams: 4–10 seconds or long enough to read;
- emotional reaction: 4–12 seconds;
- revelatory hold: longer when earned.

Cut when the idea, power relationship, evidence, speaker, emotion, or viewer
comprehension changes—not to create arbitrary motion.

At least every 20–45 seconds, introduce a meaningful new presentation mode or
story function.

All existing Celeb Workout hygiene gates remain binding:

- ≥60% training/physique footage among footage beats where the format ledger
  requires it;
- no source window crosses a shot boundary;
- no repeated scene;
- no third-party watermarks;
- no blurred pillarbox;
- no low-resolution upscale;
- no misleading archive date;
- every final crop re-verified;
- fitness cards use real evidence/photos and appear only in the contiguous
  Protocol Act.

## 13. Graphic system

- Primary condensed display face: Anton.
- Channel brand: red `#E3120B`, black, and white.
- MrBeast subject accent may supplement but never replace channel identity.
- Define one lower-third, date/location treatment, source label, timeline,
  medical-context card, metric card, and chapter chip.
- One conclusion per data graphic.
- Emphasis captions: 2–8 words.
- Lower third: name plus one relevant credential.
- Selective emphasis only; no competing animated word carpet.
- All central medical claims show their authoritative source.
- No emojis or fabricated diagrams.

## 14. Retention audit

Review the locked radio edit and final timeline in 30-second blocks. Record:

- current viewer question;
- new information;
- emotional change;
- evidence shown/heard;
- forward pull;
- repeated idea, if any;
- whether music reflects the story state;
- whether 10–20% can be removed without losing meaning.

Automatic red flags:

- no new information;
- no emotional or status change;
- repeated explanation;
- generic B-roll;
- static music without development;
- redundant quotations;
- history without present relevance;
- already-answered question treated as unresolved.

No final delivery until every flagged block is revised or explicitly justified.

## 15. Three-level ending

### Factual payoff

State the verified protocol and result without inventing a workout split or
diet and without suggesting a Crohn's cure.

### Character payoff

The transformation shows how Jimmy uses contracts, stakes, environment,
friendship, and extreme consistency to create control where motivation alone
would fail.

### Universal meaning

Control cannot guarantee health. But vulnerability does not eliminate agency.
The most meaningful transformation is not defeating the body; it is rebuilding
a relationship with it.

Return to the teenage image from the opening. It should now read not as a
before-photo, but as evidence of a private struggle the audience could not yet
see.

Finish with a brief thematic bridge to the next celebrity story. Do not append
a generic spoken like/subscribe monologue that breaks the emotional resolution.
