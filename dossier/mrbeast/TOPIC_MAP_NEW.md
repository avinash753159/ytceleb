## Factual flags

**The "40% body fat" figure — unchanged and still Airrack's.** Nothing in these three sources touches body composition. There is no passage here that could be misread as Jimmy claiming it. The existing manifest record `airrack_jimmy_41_percent` (`7r3ORKgNUjw` 510.24) remains the only place the figure appears, it is frame-verified as Eric/Airrack speaking, and it says **41%**, not 40%. Whatever number the film uses, it must be attributed to Airrack.

**Two new misread risks introduced by these sources:**

- `NdjcGrpNSF4` 1321.24 — *"Tastes cheap. I would never eat that. Ew. I have zero desire to consume any more of this."* This is Jimmy tasting **competitor candy for Feastables product research** at midnight. It is not about Crohn's trigger foods. Placed anywhere near the diet beat it will read as a health statement, and it is not one.
- `NdjcGrpNSF4` 1297.6 — the "50% more energy" line is **not Jimmy**. If mis-attributed it destroys the illness chapter's premise.

**A chronology problem the film should resolve.** In this 115-minute interview Jimmy twice dates the all-in decision to **age 13** (`4057.0`: "ever since i was 13 i just made the decision i was going to be youtuber i was going to die trying") and describes himself at **15** with no mention of illness (`1275.04`: "i'm a dumb 15 year old"). The college dropout — the actual burning of the alternative — happens **after high school**, so roughly age 18. The Crohn's diagnosis at 15 therefore sits *between* a decision already made at 13 and a bridge burned at 18. The DOACEO bite already in the manifest (`doaceo_weight_collapse`) compresses this into "I'm not playing baseball in college anymore. So then I was like, it's just all in on YouTube." Both accounts are his; they are not identical. If the origin chapter implies the diagnosis *caused* the decision, this source contradicts it.

**Baseball is corroborated, but only by the hosts.** `c8VcUnz3nVc` 708.0–719.84 — "everywhere you drive it's like a baseball field ... we met someone today and they said they played baseball with you." Independent corroboration that he played, and a licence to cut to baseball-field B-roll. Host-spoken. Do not attribute to Jimmy.
