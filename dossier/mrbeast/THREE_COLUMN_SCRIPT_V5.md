# The Disease That Built MrBeast | His Real Workout & Diet

Three-column radio script — Version 5. Emotional rewrite on recovered evidence.

## Thesis (one line, everything serves it)

**The most energetic man on the internet has almost no energy.** Everything
Jimmy built, he built while his own immune system was attacking him — and the
workout was never vanity. It was a man taking something back from a body that
had been taking things from him since he was fifteen.

That single line resolves the three questions that were competing for the
film: it is **man versus disease**, it is **willpower**, and it is **defying
the odds** — because they are the same story once you know he was doing all of
it exhausted.

## What changed from V4

**Evidence recovered.** The original research concluded "no verified diet
exists in the public record." That was wrong. Rogan #1788 at ~87–91 minutes
contains a full first-person account that no prior pass had transcribed:

| New material | What it unlocks |
|---|---|
| "I'm probably one of the least energetic people you'll ever meet." | The thesis, in his own voice |
| "Sometimes I'll flare up and then I'm just like, I'm dead. I just lay in bed all day." | The emotional floor — what the disease actually costs |
| "I'm on what's called Remicade… every eight weeks they do an IV." | The treatment, specifically |
| "Corn. Anything spicy. Anything overly processed." | His actual trigger foods — a real, sourced diet |
| "It's just one of those things — I'm so used to it. It's just life." | The ending |

**Cuts corrected.** `airrack_contract_premise` ran past its idea into "we have
to compete in an actual bodybuilding competition" — a different story, never
paid off, and it reframed a health film as a contest. Cut at 5.95s.
`airrack_jimmy_continue` opened on the host's question; it now opens on Jimmy.

## Director's notes applied

1. **Feel before you understand.** V4 opened by explaining a method. V5 opens
   on a contradiction you feel in four seconds.
2. **Every scene turns.** No act ends in the state it began.
3. **The specific is universal.** Not "a restricted diet" — corn, and taking
   the buns off a burger. Not "fatigue" — lying in bed all day.
4. **Withhold the thesis.** The title question is not answered until the last
   ninety seconds.
5. **Do not score the saddest moment.** Act 3 is the sparsest cue in the film.
6. **Contrast the scale.** The largest audience on earth, against one man who
   can't get out of bed.
7. **Narration must not echo the clip.** Enforced by `pipeline/echo_check.py`.

Evidence labels: **JIMMY'S ACCOUNT** · **MEDICAL CONTEXT** (NIDDK / Crohn's &
Colitis Foundation) · **FILM'S INTERPRETATION**. No picture is authorised by
this file.

## Locks

- Never say Crohn's caused his success. The film explicitly rejects it.
- Never present exercise, or any diet, as a treatment or cure for Crohn's.
- Never call the pact 310 or 602 consecutive workouts; programmed rest counted.
- Never attribute Airrack's 41% body-fat figure to Jimmy.
- Never invent a split, sets, reps, calories or a meal plan.
- Remicade and the trigger foods are **his account**, not medical guidance.
- Every used bite must be transcript-and-frame verified.
- Full audio approval required before any visual asset acquisition.
