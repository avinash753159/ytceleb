## Executive verdict

Keep the current **control versus vulnerability** thesis, but rebuild the first
90 seconds around the **contract anomaly**, then reveal the teenage Crohn's
collapse as the reason that anomaly matters. This hybrid wins because it makes
the workout story begin immediately while preserving the deeper emotional
meaning.

The current architecture is thoughtful, unusually careful with medical claims,
and has a real ending. Its weakness is that it asks a fitness audience to wait
roughly 3:06 before reaching the machine chapter and roughly 4:59 before the
contract chapter. The opening also explains the theme before the viewer has
experienced enough evidence to form the question themselves.

The best revision is not a wholesale rewrite. It is a reorder and compression:

1. Open on day 310, the tattoo stakes, and the two-hour daily cost.
2. Ask why the world's most optimization-obsessed creator needed another person
   to make him care for his own body.
3. Reveal `190 → 139`, Crohn's, and the lost baseball future.
4. Show how the YouTube control machine grew from the terrain that remained.
5. Return to the contract, then deliver the verified protocol.
6. Let the disappointing progress reveal supply the reversal.
7. Resolve on agency rather than cure or bodily mastery.

## Current architecture: independent critique

### What works

- The central contradiction is excellent: a man who can control vast
  productions cannot command his immune system.
- The script refuses the lazy claim that Crohn's "caused" MrBeast's success.
- It distinguishes the day-310 account, the later 600-day challenge, programmed
  rest, 15,000 steps in one interview, and 12,500 in a separate post.
- It explicitly refuses to invent a split, exercise list, calorie target, meal
  plan, trainer identity, or cure narrative.
- Primary voices carry confession, pain, ambition, accountability, difficulty,
  disappointment, and gratitude.
- Chapter 5 is a genuine reversal: consistency produces progress, not the
  fantasy of controlling biology.
- Chapter 6 answers the factual, character, and universal questions and returns
  to the teenage image.
- The sound plan uses subtraction, room tone, J/L cuts, motivated cue changes,
  and restraint rather than constant "epic" scoring.

### What weakens retention

- The first line, "Look at him for a moment," depends on a visually compelling
  and securely dated teenage shot that is still listed as unverified.
- The opening states the interpretation ("turned that machinery back on
  himself") before fully dramatizing the contract.
- Three Crohn's/weight bites occur early, followed by another illness-heavy
  origin section. The facts are important, but the experience risks feeling
  like two introductions.
- The contract—the cleanest visual anomaly and most concrete stakes—does not
  become the main action until minute five.
- The "machine" chapter contains five interviews and abstract production
  analysis in 1:53. It risks becoming a podcast montage unless every visual
  proves a distinct operational claim.
- The useful protocol section begins after approximately 57% of the film.
- The protocol is honest but thin relative to the title phrase "His Real
  Workout & Diet." Viewers learn categories, not a workout or diet they can
  perform. This is the evidence ceiling, but packaging must set that expectation.
- The ending makes the control/vulnerability point in several adjacent forms:
  "cannot promise the outcome," "control cannot guarantee health," and
  "vulnerability does not eliminate agency." One clean formulation will land
  harder than three.

### Truth and framing risks

- The title **The Disease That Built MrBeast** implies stronger causality than
  the script can prove and then carefully disclaims. The contradiction can
  drive a click, but the first minute must qualify it explicitly.
- "The most productive creator alive" is an unprovable superlative. Replace with
  a concrete description such as "a creator running productions with hundreds
  of people and multimillion-dollar stakes," if sourced.
- "The teenager who could not control what happened inside his own body" is
  fair thematic framing; "turned that machinery back on himself" is inference
  and should be presented as the film's reading, not Jimmy's stated motive.
- "The channel had consumed too much of his health" overreaches the verified
  quote, which says he realized he had not been working out or taking care of
  himself. Use his narrower formulation.
- "Food management" and "coaching" need direct source support if included in the
  factual final summary. The current bank proves food consumed time, but does
  not yet prove a particular nutrition strategy. It also does not identify or
  quote a coach.
- The `41%` window contains an ambiguous pronoun/context problem already noted
  in the bank. Do not build a graphic until the adjacent sequence establishes
  unambiguously that Jimmy is speaking about himself.
- Reusing `doaceo_treatment_burden` in Chapters 2 and 5 is explicitly offered as
  an alternate in the script, but the measured cutlist uses it twice. Remove one
  use.

## Tournament pitches

### Candidate A — Current: “The Body He Could Not Control”

**Hook:** teenage image → 190-to-139 collapse → pain → modern production control
→ daily protocol.

**Architecture:** illness origin → YouTube machine → contract → protocol →
biological limit → agency.

**Strength:** deepest emotional interpretation and best circular ending.

**Weakness:** starts like an illness profile and postpones the workout engine.

### Candidate B — “The Contract MrBeast Couldn’t Edit”

**0:00–0:07:** Day 310. Jimmy says he and Eric signed a contract to work out
every day or get tattoos of each other.

**0:07–0:20:** Airrack adds the forehead penalty and later 600-day scale.

**0:20–0:36:** Jimmy says the routine costs an hour and a half, food, 15,000
steps, and at least two hours taken from the channel.

**0:36–0:52:** Establish the anomaly: why did someone who can optimize almost
anything need a punishment and a witness to protect two hours for himself?

**0:52–1:15:** Flash `190 → 139`, baseball ending, stabbing pain, giant sets,
day 310, and the later admission that progress was far smaller than expected.

**Central question:** What was the contract really designed to overcome—and
what could it never control?

**Chapters:**

1. The contract (stakes and immediate action)
2. The body that broke the first routine (Crohn's/baseball)
3. The machine that replaced it (YouTube obsession and cost)
4. The real protocol (lifting, movement, time, food evidence limits)
5. The disappointing result (progress versus fantasy)
6. What he reclaimed (agency, not cure)

**Strength:** strongest click-to-content match, fastest workout relevance, and
best question chain.

**Risk:** the illness reveal can feel like a manufactured twist unless the
transition is compassionate and non-causal.

### Candidate C — “Two Transformations: 190→139→41%”

**Hook:** On one side, a 15-year-old loses 51 pounds because of Crohn's. Years
later, Jimmy says he begins a fitness challenge at 41% body fat. Same person;
opposite-looking body crises; neither can be understood from a photograph.

**Architecture:** involuntary transformation → lost athletic identity → work
obsession and rebound → chosen transformation → verified protocol → incomplete
measurements → sustainable agency.

**Central question:** Can a chosen transformation repair what an involuntary
one took away?

**Strength:** visually legible, highly emotional, and naturally makes metrics
part of an evidence investigation.

**Risk:** the 41% context and later body-fat result are not yet secure enough.
It can also over-center weight and invite misleading before/after treatment.

### Candidate D — “The Two Machines”

**Hook:** Intercut a MrBeast control room with Jimmy describing an immune system
attacking his digestive tract. One machine responds to attention; the other
does not.

**Architecture:** body system → YouTube system → accountability system →
training system → limits of systems → chosen action.

**Central question:** What happens when the world's most obsessive optimizer
meets a problem that cannot be optimized away?

**Strength:** most distinctive essay concept and strongest thematic unity.

**Risk:** abstract, less workout-forward, dependent on superb visual
juxtaposition, and vulnerable to the same delayed-action problem as V1.

## Judge panel

### Retention editor

Winner: **B**. It supplies an immediately understandable visual stake, a
recognizable subject, a punishment, a measurable duration, and a mystery. It
also lets the film pay off a fitness question every two to three minutes rather
than spending five minutes establishing theme.

### Investigative editor

Winner: **A/D tie**, with a condition. Both handle inference and medical
uncertainty responsibly. B wins overall only if it preserves the explicit
distinctions among the initial pact, day 310, programmed rest, and the later
600-day framing.

### Fitness-channel editor

Winner: **B**. The contract and verified protocol arrive early. However, the
title should promise an investigation into the "real" routine, not imply a full
replicable workout and diet plan that the evidence does not contain.

### Character/story editor

Winner: **A**, narrowly over D. The teenage-image callback and agency-not-cure
resolution are the film's most memorable material. B should inherit this
ending rather than substitute a conventional transformation reveal.

### Standards/completeness editor

Winner: **B**, after revision. It most naturally satisfies anomaly-first
opening, fitness-first pictures, chapter question chains, primary-source
interludes, and partial payoffs. No candidate is production-ready until the
claim holds and timing defects below are cleared.

### Panel decision

Adopt **B's order**, **A's emotional spine and ending**, and **D's two-system
visual motif**. Reject C as the primary structure unless the original body-fat
and transformation records are secured; its metrics are too fragile to carry
the film.

## Precise revision order

### 1. Recut the first 90 seconds

Use this evidence order:

1. `colin_day_310_contract` — begin at the first complete sentence that lands
   "day 310" and the tattoo accountability.
2. `airrack_tattoo_stakes` — use only enough to clarify the punishment; identify
   that this is later challenge framing.
3. `colin_daily_protocol` — retain the hour-and-a-half, food, and 15,000 steps.
4. Narration question: "Why did a creator who measures everything need another
   person to make sure he took care of himself?"
5. `doaceo_weight_collapse` — reveal 190 to 139 and baseball ending.
6. Very short `doaceo_pain` excerpt—one vivid phrase, not the whole 10.94
   seconds.
7. Modern production-control montage.
8. `airrack_jimmy_progress_reality` excerpt as the reversal promise.
9. Thesis: the system changed his behavior, not the fact of vulnerability.

Target 75–90 seconds. Do not play three full illness bites in this window.

### 2. Compress origin and machine into one causal chapter

Target 2:00–2:30 combined, down from 3:14.

- Keep baseball, Crohn's diagnosis, channel archive, decade of obsession, and
  social cost.
- Use one clinical definition card and one first-person symptom bite.
- Keep the line "The camera stayed."
- Cut either `doaceo_extreme_ambition` or `doaceo_best_at_everything`; they
  perform similar character work.
- Replace abstract repetition with a causal bridge: illness closed baseball;
  YouTube absorbed time; obsession built control; that control later crowded
  out self-care.
- Do not imply Crohn's mechanically created his ambition.

### 3. Bring the actionable protocol forward

Enter the protocol by approximately 35–40% of runtime, not 57%.

Organize the section around four verified levers:

1. **Accountability:** partner, consequence, programmed plan/rest.
2. **Resistance training:** consistent coached training, with no invented split.
3. **Daily movement:** 15,000 at the June 2023 interview; 12,500 only if the
   original post is captured.
4. **Protected time:** hour-and-a-half workout; at least two total hours no
   longer going to the main channel.

Treat food as an evidence boundary, not a full lever, until a primary source
proves the management method. A useful line is: "We can verify that food added
time and planning. We cannot verify a complete menu or calorie target."

Add one viewer-useful inference card, clearly labeled **Principle, not
prescription**:

- Make the behavior observable.
- Budget time before motivation disappears.
- Track adherence, not a fantasy deadline.
- Use programmed rest rather than treating recovery as failure.

This improves workout usefulness without pretending Jimmy published a program.

### 4. Make the disappointing-progress bite the major reversal

Move `airrack_jimmy_progress_reality` to the 65–75% range and give it space.
It should change the meaning of the contract: success is no longer achieving a
fantasy physique on schedule; it is continuing after the fantasy collapses.

Follow it with the "three months becomes a habit" bite or the changed-life bite,
not more Crohn's exposition. The medical limit has already been established.

### 5. Tighten the ending

Retain:

- `airrack_jimmy_changed_life`
- factual summary with evidence caveat
- teenage-image callback
- `airrack_jimmy_continue`

Condense the adjacent thesis lines to one:

> The contract could not make his body predictable. It gave him something
> smaller and more durable: a way to keep choosing it.

Then return to the teenage image and distinguish illness from weakness. Avoid
repeating "control," "vulnerability," and "agency" as explicit abstractions in
three consecutive narration blocks.

Replace the generic next-video bridge only after the next subject is known. If
none is selected at lock, end on Jimmy's nearly dry source line and a restrained
channel end screen rather than a placeholder promise.

### 6. Reconcile packaging with evidence

Preferred title if the existing title remains:

**The Disease That Built MrBeast | His Real Workout System**

This is more accurate than "Workout & Diet" because the public record supports
an accountability/time/training system but not a complete diet.

If "Workout & Diet" must remain, the opening must explicitly promise:
"We reconstructed everything the public evidence can prove—and found the meal
plan everyone repeats is not actually public." The absence becomes part of the
investigation, not a disappointing omission.

## Definition of “story phase complete”

The story phase is complete only when:

- the contract-led opening is measured at 75–90 seconds;
- the protocol begins by 40% of runtime;
- every two to four minutes contains a meaningful partial payoff;
- all claims in narration map to a primary or authoritative source, or are
  explicitly labeled inference;
- no selected quotation is repeated;
- every selected source window is transcript- and frame-verified;
- the title's workout/diet promise matches the evidence actually delivered;
- the final teenage-image callback answers the opening without claiming disease
  caused success or exercise cured illness;
- the revised radio edit passes a 30-second retention audit and hard sync gate.
