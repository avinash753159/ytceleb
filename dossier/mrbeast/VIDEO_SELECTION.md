# Video Selection — The Disease That Built MrBeast

30 of 105 outstanding cutaway shots get generated video (Veo, ~$0.30/shot) instead of the
default still + depth push-in (~$0.04/shot). Test applied to every shot: **does motion carry
meaning that a still cannot?** 30 video + 75 still = $12.00, leaving $5.44 for re-rolls.

Full list: `manifest/video_shots.json`

## Held back on purpose

Several strong candidates lost out to the 30-shot cap and stayed stills:

- **s066a / s066c** (same beat as s066b, abandoned-diamond decay) — the chosen s066b has walls actively narrowing during the shot; a/c are static environmental portraits that a push-in flatters fine.
- **s072b** (aerial rising off the giant set) — same segment as s072d; only the stronger closing image (72d) earned the spend.
- **s009c** (monitor flickering to life) — the flicker is too brief to justify 7.5x cost; push-in on the glow works.
- **s029a, s047b, s070a** — machine tracking shot, drawer of blank cards, dead mirror rig: each is a composition whose value is the composition, exactly what the still + push-in is for.

## What I wanted but couldn't fit

**s054b** (diagram's third node replaced by inflamed tissue) and **s014a/s014b** (camera passing
through tissue layers; cells doing their real job) were close calls in the disease-mechanism block —
genuinely process-driven, but the block already has five video shots (14c, 15a, 15b, 15c, 16b, 16c)
and adding more would have crowded a single explainer segment at the expense of the emotional
beats later in the film (56a/c, 63a, 66b, 72d). If a re-roll leaves headroom in the $5.44 buffer,
s014a is the next one I'd upgrade.
