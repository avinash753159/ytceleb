# The Disease That Built MrBeast | His Real Workout & Diet

Status: primary-source research in progress  
Subject: Jimmy Donaldson / MrBeast  
Format: V11 archival documentary  
Working thesis: He spent his teens unable to control his own body. The later
fitness transformation was a deliberate attempt to reclaim it using the same
systems, stakes, and obsessive consistency that built his channel.

Binding story, retention, sound, and delivery requirements are defined in
`dossier/mrbeast/PRODUCTION_STANDARD.md`. The film is not considered complete
merely because it renders; it must also pass that document's radio-edit,
chapter-chain, retention, ending, and click-package gates.

## Evidence rules

- A spoken claim is not cleared until its exact source window is verified.
- Medical explanation must be attributed to Jimmy or to an authoritative
  medical source; the film must not turn his account into medical advice.
- YouTube auto-captions are discovery aids only. Final quotations will be
  checked against downloaded source audio and punctuated with faster-whisper.
- The picture in every selected window must be inspected after crop treatment.
- The film will distinguish the 12,500-step figure Jimmy posted publicly from
  the 15,000-step figure he gave in the June 2023 Colin and Samir interview.

## Claims held pending stronger primary evidence

- “40%+ to sub-20% body fat.” A reliable exact first-person window has not yet
  been isolated.
- “12,500 steps every day.” This appears in Jimmy's June 29, 2023 social post;
  the original post or an archived capture must be acquired before narration.
- The detailed lifting split, exercises, sets, reps, and diet. Do not infer a
  conventional bodybuilding program from third-party fitness articles.
- The identity of “Eric” and the exact exceptions allowed by the contract.
- “Thirty pounds in one summer.” Current first-person evidence is stronger and
  says roughly fifty-one pounds (190 to 139); the script should use Jimmy's own
  later account unless the earlier claim is independently sourced.

## Medical context required

Authoritative context selected for the script:

- NIDDK definition and facts:
  https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/crohns-disease/definition-facts
  — Crohn's is a chronic inflammatory bowel disease involving abnormal immune
  reactions and digestive-tract inflammation; symptoms and severity vary.
- NIDDK symptoms and causes:
  https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/crohns-disease/symptoms-causes
  — common symptoms include diarrhea, abdominal pain/cramping, and weight loss.
- NIDDK nutrition:
  https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/digestive-diseases/crohns-disease/eating-diet-nutrition
  — nutrition must be individualized with a clinician.
- Crohn's & Colitis Foundation:
  https://www.crohnscolitisfoundation.org/patientsandcaregivers/justlikeme/treatment/food
  — no particular food or diet is known to cause, prevent, or cure IBD.

Required script language: Jimmy's workout is biography, not a Crohn's treatment
and not medical advice.

## Story architecture

1. **The Kid Who Was Disappearing** — adolescent archive and the unexplained
   physical change.
2. **Ten Times a Day** — Jimmy's first-person Crohn's account.
3. **The Machine** — the control systems and obsession that built the channel.
4. **The Contract** — day 310 and the tattoo stakes.
5. **The Protocol** — only what primary evidence supports: lifting, high daily
   movement, food/time commitment, consistency.
6. **The Cost of Control** — illness remains; fitness did not “defeat” Crohn's.
7. **Reclaiming the Body** — payoff to the opening promise.
