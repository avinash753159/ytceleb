# MrBeast V3 — Audio-First Documentary Standard

Status: audio development only. Picture production is blocked until the user
explicitly approves the full audio master.

## What changes from the rejected cut

The rejected film delivered information as a continuous listicle. The remake
will be an investigation driven by testimony, contradiction, and consequence.
Fast cutting is not the missing ingredient: sampled CelebWorkout uploads
already average roughly 1.9–2.7 seconds per shot. The missing ingredients are
story selection, causal visuals, source quality, and audio escalation.

Patrick Gavia's useful grammar is adapted, not copied:

- begin with a human wound or contradiction, not a summary;
- let primary-source testimony carry the story;
- pose a question whose answer changes during the film;
- use silence as punctuation and hold the consequence of a quote;
- change musical language at act turns rather than looping one bed;
- reveal documents, dates, and qualifications as evidence;
- reserve the most damaging contradiction for a later reversal;
- end on a changed interpretation, not a motivational recap.

Do not copy Patrick's captions, graphics, watermark, presenter staging, music,
or channel identity.

## Story thesis

Crohn's did not create MrBeast. In Jimmy Donaldson's own account, it ended his
baseball path and redirected him toward YouTube. The system he later built
could make training observable and consistent, but it could not make his body
predictable or guarantee the result.

The title is a provocation that the film must correct in the opening minute:
the evidence supports redirection, not literal causation.

## Central question

If the accountability pact could not guarantee a transformation, what did it
actually give him?

## Five-act radio arc

1. **The path that closed** — illness, baseball, and the move all-in on
   YouTube, explicitly presented as Jimmy's account.
2. **The machine** — obsession becomes a production system while self-care
   becomes a cost.
3. **The pact** — the verified workout system: programmed resistance training,
   accountability, daily movement, and protected time. The complete split,
   calories, and meal plan are not public.
4. **The fantasy breaks** — the long challenge cannot force progress on the
   imagined schedule.
5. **The machine wins again** — Jimmy later says travel, Beast Games, workload,
   and lost sleep displaced training. The durable lesson is not perfect
   adherence; it is the recurring fight to reserve time for an unpredictable
   body.

## Voice and testimony

- Target 55–65% primary-source speech, not a continuous narration river.
- Narration should connect, interrogate, and qualify; it should not repeat
  what the bite just said.
- Preserve breaths and source-room tails when emotionally useful.
- Do not slice a quote so tightly that the speaker sounds clipped or coerced.
- Every medical description from Jimmy must remain clearly his first-person
  account. General medical context requires an authoritative source.

## Score palette

All current music is from Scott Buckley's CC BY 4.0 library and requires
description credit.

- `intervention_nomelody.mp3` — unresolved investigation and interior conflict.
- `descent.mp3` — illness, fatigue, and loss of control; use sparingly.
- `red_no_vocals.mp3` — the production machine and accountability pact.
- `celestial.mp3` — earned expansion after the pact becomes habit.
- `chasing_daylight.mp3` — reflective reversal and resolution.

Music rules:

- no music under the opening confession unless the quote needs a nearly
  inaudible room-support tone;
- dialogue remains at least 10–12 dB above the score during bites;
- stop the score completely after consequential phrases;
- no medical heartbeat, monitor beep, trailer braam, or comedy sting;
- use one restrained sub impact only at a true act turn;
- automate level changes; never lay one generic loop under the whole film.

## Mix and approval gates

- 48 kHz stereo WAV master plus a 192–256 kbps MP3 review copy.
- Dialogue-first mix, approximately -14 LUFS integrated and no higher than
  -2 dBTP after final encoding checks.
- Natural room-tone bridges must prevent obvious noise-floor jumps between
  podcast sources.
- Deliver a time-coded cue sheet and license/attribution ledger.
- First approval: 60–90 second cold-open prototype.
- Second approval: full radio cut.
- Only after explicit second approval: write `audio_lock.json` with the WAV's
  SHA-256 and allow picture work to begin.

## Non-negotiable claim locks

- Never say Crohn's caused Jimmy's success.
- Never present exercise as a Crohn's treatment or cure.
- Never call the pact 310 or 602 consecutive workouts; programmed rest was
  allowed.
- Never attribute Airrack's 41% body-fat statement to Jimmy.
- Never invent Jimmy's exercise split, sets, reps, calories, meals, or coach.
- Keep changing figures dated and labeled as self-reports.
- The later “something had to give” admission is a change in circumstances,
  not proof that the earlier system was fraudulent.
