# Cold-Open Tournament

These treatments are audio-only. No picture directions authorize visual
production.

## A — “Probably a blessing”

**Question:** How could the disease Jimmy calls a blessing also become the body
limit his systems could not overcome?

1. Narrator introduces Jimmy's “probably a blessing” statement.
2. Verified JRE bite: Crohn's ended the baseball focus and he went all-in on
   YouTube.
3. Narrator immediately rejects the title's literal causal reading.
4. Verified JRE bite: a decade of YouTube obsession.
5. Verified Colin and Samir bite: he realized he had stopped taking care of
   himself.
6. Narrator: the answer was accountability with stakes.
7. Full verified day-310 pact bite, including the programmed-rest
   qualification.
8. Music stops.
9. Partial verified Airrack bite: “I can't believe how little progress I've
   made in the amount of time.”
10. Title and unresolved central question.

**Strengths:** unique direct quote, title tension, strong causal correction,
high primary-source ratio, and a clean result-versus-process contradiction.

**Risks:** “once I got that” in the JRE bite needs the narrator's antecedent;
the profanity may require a tasteful censor; the title correction cannot be
softened.

## B — “The body he couldn't optimize”

**Question:** What happens when the internet's most relentless optimizer meets
a system that does not obey attention?

1. Verified DOACEO bite: “Does physics allow it? Then yes, it's possible.”
2. Narrator contrasts the controllable production machine with the body.
3. Verified JRE bite: Jimmy says he has almost no energy and is easily drained.
4. Verified day-310 pact bite.
5. Verified Airrack progress admission.
6. Title.

**Strengths:** immediately defines the optimizer-versus-biology conflict.

**Risks:** delays the human origin wound, can feel like a thesis essay, and
needs careful handling of Jimmy's simplified medical explanation.

## C — “Something had to give”

**Question:** Why did a successful long-term fitness pact later disappear from
Jimmy's life?

1. Dry verified DOACEO bite: “Something had to give, and sadly it was working
   out.”
2. Narrator rewinds from the collapse to the disease and baseball.
3. Verified origin bite.
4. Verified day-310 pact bite.
5. Narrator promises to reconstruct the real protocol and the forces that
   broke it.

**Strengths:** strongest present-day reversal and clearest narrative mystery.

**Risks:** spends the film's best late reveal in the first seconds and can make
the earlier progress feel pointless before the viewer understands it.

## Provisional winner

Treatment A. It supplies the human wound before the system, corrects the
click-driven title, and ends on a verified contradiction without spending the
later “something had to give” reversal. Treatment C is retained as the Act 5
turn, not the cold open.
