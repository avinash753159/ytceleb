# JASON STATHAM — TRAINING & PHYSIQUE FACT DOSSIER
For: "Train Like Jason Statham" (~12 min documentary)
Compiled: 2026-07-26. Every claim carries a source URL + confidence tag.
Tags: **CONFIRMED** = primary source or multiple reputable sources · **PLAUSIBLE** = single source, not contradicted · items that could not be verified are flagged **UNVERIFIED — DROP**.

Key correction vs. the brief: the famous Men's Health training diary is **NOT circa 2010–2011**. The original article was posted **September 6, 2007** on menshealth.com (US), by **Mike Zimmerman**, photos by Art Streiber — it circulated widely again in 2010–2011, which is why it is often misdated. Full original text recovered via the Wayback Machine (see §1). Also: Logan Hood's company is **"Epoch Training"** (epochtraining.com), not "Epoch Rising."

## 1. THE TRAINING SYSTEM — the Men's Health diary (2007)

### The article itself
- **CONFIRMED (primary, full text recovered):** "Jason Statham's Weight Loss Workout / Statham's Secrets of Superlean," by Mike Zimmerman, photographs by Art Streiber, posted Sept 6, 2007, menshealth.com (US).
  - Original URL: `http://www.menshealth.com/fitness/jason-stathams-weight-loss-workout` (dead; menshealth.com now blocks crawlers)
  - Working archived copy (pages 1–4): https://web.archive.org/web/20101127045132/http://www.menshealth.com/fitness/jason-stathams-weight-loss-workout (append `/page/2`, `/page/3`, `/page/4`)
  - Companion page (merged with a ~2006 MH interview, "Statham's Fitness Tips"): https://web.archive.org/web/20100605015200/http://www.menshealth.com/celebrity-fitness/jason-statham-superlean-workout
- **CONFIRMED:** The magazine version was an **October (2007) cover feature**; MH's editors posted "The Complete Jason Statham Workout" (the full 7-day diary week) online because the print version was edited down. Editor's note preserved in the PDF copy: https://pdfcoffee.com/the-complete-jason-statham-workout-pdf-free.html ; also mirrored at https://www.scribd.com/document/80563492/The-Complete-Jason-Statham-Workout
- **CONFIRMED:** Modern republication of the full week: Men's Health Australia, "Jason Statham's Complete Workout Routine" — https://menshealth.com.au/jason-statham-workout-routine/ (site blocks fetch, URL verified via search)

### Who ran it
- **CONFIRMED (primary):** Trainer = **Logan Hood, former US Navy SEAL, founder of Epoch Training (epochtraining.com), Los Angeles**. Exact article text: "He works out every day but Sunday with Logan Hood, a former Navy SEAL that runs Epoch Training… Saturdays are reserved for hour long sustained trail runs in the Hollywood Hills while the other 5 days are spent at 87Eleven, a full service action film company and stunt studio located in a converted warehouse near the Los Angeles airport." (Wayback link above)
- **CONFIRMED:** The transformation: **17 lb lost in 6 weeks, training 6 days/week, ≤ ~35 min/day**. Statham verbatim: "That's 17 pounds in 6 weeks, mate… And that's working out 6 days a week for, at most, about 35 minutes a day. I've never, ever gotten results like this before." (Wayback, p.1)
- **CONFIRMED:** Context: he'd gotten "a bit lardy" filming *War* (2007, with Jet Li); the 6-week program came immediately before/around *Death Race* (filmed 2007, released 2008). Sources: Wayback article p.1 + https://www.fitnessandpower.com/training/workout-routines/jason-statham-workout-routine + https://physicalstrategies.medium.com/jason-statham-evolution-of-fitness-755652bbbd88
- **PLAUSIBLE:** Hood's method descended from Mark Twight's Gym Jones system (the *300* cast gym). Source: https://physicalstrategies.medium.com/jason-statham-evolution-of-fitness-755652bbbd88 (also echoed in Men's Journal search snippets)

### The two rules (primary text)
- **CONFIRMED:** Rule 1 — **No repeats.** Statham: "I haven't had one single day in 6 weeks that has been a repeat… you'll never do that workout you did on Thursday the 23rd of August again. It always changes, and that's what keeps it so interesting."
- **CONFIRMED:** Rule 2 — **Record everything.** All work tracked; heavy lifts logged so percentages can be calculated for later workouts. Statham: "If you want to get faster, stronger, and healthier you have to record and track progress."
  (Both: Wayback p.2, link above)

### Session architecture (primary text)
- **CONFIRMED:** Every session = 3 stages: (1) **10-min warm-up on a Concept2 rower** ("low-impact and works the cardiovascular system"); (2) **10 min medium intensity** — one of: heavy compound lifts (front squat / deadlift / power clean, "never more than five reps at a time"), light-weight circuits, kettlebell/sandbag carries, a ~15-exercise kettlebell progression, or medicine-ball throws; (3) **interval training** — the brutal finisher that "blows every gasket… You're crying for air. It redlines the heart into oblivion." (Wayback p.2)
- **CONFIRMED:** His circuit exercise menu (pick 6, run circuit 5×): ball slams 20 reps (20-lb ball), 25-ft rope climbs ×5 (arms only), pullups ×8 (incl. bar-to-bar "Dyno" jumps), rope "snappers/whip smashes" ×20, hanging knee raises ×20, burpees ×20, bear crawl (basketball court, 3× up-and-back), farmer walks ×3 lengths, front squats ×20, rope pulls (plates tied to 50-ft rope) ×5, weighted stepups ×20. (Wayback p.4)
- **CONFIRMED:** Big-lift protocol: bench/squat/deadlift at ~75% 1RM, never inside circuits. Statham: "My workout always changes. That keeps it interesting." (Wayback p.4)
- **CONFIRMED:** **Front squat strength: 5 reps at 1.25× bodyweight** — "Statham can do five at 1.25 times his body weight." (Wayback p.4)

### The published 7-day diary week (from "The Complete Jason Statham Workout")
All numbers below appear consistently in the PDF copy of MH's online companion and in the MH Australia republication + Fitness and Power write-up:
- **CONFIRMED — Day 1 (deadlift day):** 10-min row warm-up (~20 strokes/min) → pyramid circuit of pushups/ring pullups/bodyweight squats (1-to-5 rep ladder) → **9 progressive deadlift sets: 135 lb → 185 → 235 → 285 → then singles 325, 340, 350, 360, finishing at 365 lb (~165 kg) near-max single** → cool-down on Olympic trampoline.
- **CONFIRMED — Day 2 ("Big Five 55"):** static-hold warm-up (ring dips, kettlebell farmer holds, L-sits, bodyweight squat hold) → 5 exercises, descending ladder 10-9-8…-1 = **55 total reps each, no rest**: front squats, pullups, decline pushups, power cleans, knees-to-elbows.
- **CONFIRMED — Day 3 (rowing intervals):** six 500 m sprints on the Concept2, 3-min rests; **published times 1:38.7 to 1:50.3** → cool-down 500 m farmer's walk with two 30 kg (66-lb) kettlebells.
- **CONFIRMED — Day 4 (front squat day):** 5×5 front squats (~80 kg / 1.25× BW, 90-sec rests) → 200 pushups via 13 rungs of a ladder with a partner.
- **CONFIRMED — Day 5 ("cumulative movements" circuit):** 11-exercise continuous circuit (rope climbs, front squats, ball slams, rope pulls, bench press, dips, resisted sled work, etc.) — **his logged time: 23:53**.
- **CONFIRMED — Day 6 (contextual effort):** trail run in the Hollywood Hills — **his logged run: 73 minutes**.
- **CONFIRMED — Day 7:** full rest.
  Sources: https://pdfcoffee.com/the-complete-jason-statham-workout-pdf-free.html · https://www.fitnessandpower.com/training/workout-routines/jason-statham-workout-routine · https://menshealth.com.au/jason-statham-workout-routine/
- **CONFIRMED (with caveat):** The "Big Five 55" ladder was designed by strength coach **Dan John**; MH credits him. Sources: http://gettingfitwithgabeandjoe.blogspot.com/2013/05/big-5-55-workout-and-jason-statham.html (reprints it "courtesy Dan John, from UK Men's Health") + Men's Health AU republication snippet crediting "strength coach Dan John". Caveat: Dan John contributed the workout template to MH; he was not Statham's personal coach.
- **CONFIRMED (primary):** MH's own framing of the diary: "This workout was not designed for you… just a snapshot of one week in a much longer progression." (editor's note preserved at pdfcoffee link)

### Corroborating pre-diary source
- **CONFIRMED:** Coach magazine interview (Nick Hutchings, Oct 2, 2008) independently repeats the system: 6 days/week, ~35 min, former Navy SEAL trainer, two rules (never repeat a workout, record everything), compound lifts + circuits + kettlebells + medicine balls, and Statham's tip: **"Train early in the morning. Get out of bed and just do it. That way you'll never find an excuse not to."** Also: allow **minimum 10 weeks** before a role for real change. https://www.coachweb.com/exercises/sport-workouts/359/jason-statham

## 3. BACKGROUND — DIVING & MARKET STALLS

- **CONFIRMED:** Born **26 July 1967**, Shirebrook, Derbyshire; grew up in Great Yarmouth, Norfolk. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Statham
- **CONFIRMED:** Member of Britain's national diving squad for ~a decade (Wikipedia says 12 years; Team England says ~10 — say "more than a decade" or "10+ years"). Joined a swimming club around age 11–12; inducted into the British National Diving School mid-1980s. https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Statham · https://teamengland.org/news/auckland-1990-jason-stathams-unknown-team-england-career
- **CONFIRMED:** **1990 Commonwealth Games, Auckland — competed for England: 8th in 1 m springboard, 11th in 3 m springboard, 10th/11th in 10 m platform** (Team England: 11th in platform; Wikipedia: 10th — script-safe: "8th on the 1-metre board, and outside the top ten on 3-metre and platform"). https://teamengland.org/news/auckland-1990-jason-stathams-unknown-team-england-career · https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Statham · https://www.olympics.com/en/news/actor-jason-statham-diving-career-commonwealth-games
- **CONFIRMED:** Tried for Olympic teams (Seoul '88, Barcelona '92 trials) and never made one. His own verdict: "I started too late. It probably wasn't my thing. I should have done a different sport." And: "I think what I didn't achieve in sport has probably helped me focus more and take my acting career more seriously." https://teamengland.org/news/auckland-1990-jason-stathams-unknown-team-england-career
- **PLAUSIBLE:** 12th at the 1992 World Championships (only the Furman Medium article states this; the 1991 Perth Worlds is the likely event meant — verify before using): https://physicalstrategies.medium.com/jason-statham-evolution-of-fitness-755652bbbd88
- **CONFIRMED:** Diving-era core work: "We used to do 500 situp variations every day" (primary MH text, §1 Wayback superlean page).
- **CONFIRMED (color):** Son of a street seller; worked market stalls and sold **"fake perfume and jewellery on street corners"** while training as a diver; modelling for Tommy Hilfiger, Levi's (1996), then French Connection (1997) — FC spokesperson: "We chose Jason because we wanted our model to look like a normal guy." Guy Ritchie heard about the market-stall past and cast him as the streetwise con artist Bacon in *Lock, Stock* (1998). https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Jason_Statham (MH 2006 text corroborates: "He was hustling black-market goods in London when he was typecast as a street thug in Lock, Stock…")

## 4. DIET — the documented rules (primary MH 2007 text)

- **CONFIRMED:** **~2,000 kcal/day, hard limit** ("the 2,000-calorie limit is gospel"), spread over **six small meals**. Foods: egg whites, vegetables, lean meats, fish, nuts, protein shakes.
- **CONFIRMED:** **No refined sugar or flour, ever.** No bread, no pasta, no sweets, **no fruit juice, no booze**. "That's the hardest part right there." Nightly dessert: plain yogurt with fresh fruit. (One documented cheat: a single night of beers a few weeks in.)
- **CONFIRMED:** **Log everything swallowed** — "If it goes down your throat, record it on paper." He holds up a black hardbound journal: **"This is the bible."** "Writing everything down makes it impossible for you to muck it up."
- **CONFIRMED:** **Water: ~1.5 gallons/day** ("he tries to drink 1 1/2 gallons a day — that'll keep you feeling full"). (Some later copies say 1.6 gal; use 1.5 per the primary text.)
  All above: Wayback MH pages (§1), p.3 + superlean page.
- **PLAUSIBLE (later-era rules, aggregator-sourced):** nothing after **7 p.m.**; ~95% of food "good"; no fried food; starches only earlier in the day; ~3 L water baseline. https://manofmany.com/culture/fitness/jason-statham-diet-workout-plan — the 7 p.m. rule is NOT in the 2007 MH primary text; attribute loosely ("he's said in later interviews…") or drop.
- **CONFIRMED (attitude before the diet):** "I never gave a f--k about a calorie. An apple? It's good for me. I'd have five. Bananas? Eat the bunch." (Wayback p.1)
